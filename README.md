## What it does

NPS Insight Engine turns raw survey verbatims into themes, driver analysis, and closed-loop follow-up tasks — so feedback becomes action instead of a quarterly chart.

## Key features

- **Theme clustering** — verbatims grouped into issues and praise automatically.
- **Driver analysis** — which themes move your score, quantified.
- **Closed-loop tasks** — detractor follow-ups assigned and tracked.
- **Segment cuts** — score and themes by plan, region, and tenure.
- **Trend narrated** — monthly summaries written in plain language.

## Who it's for

CX and product teams collecting feedback but drowning in verbatims.
